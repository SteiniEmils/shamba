Care

A small daily ritual.

SHAMBA is made for everyday use. A little at a time is enough.

THAAYU Body Butter

After bathing, while skin is still slightly damp, warm a small amount between your palms. Massage into dry areas — elbows, knees, shins, hands — until the butter settles. Use daily. A little goes far.

WENDO Lip Balm

Smooth a thin layer onto clean lips whenever they feel tight or dry. WENDO is meant to nourish and protect, not to shine. Carry the tin and use it as often as you need.
