THAAYU

Deep nourishment. Lasting moisture.

A rich, creamy blend that deeply moisturizes and nourishes the skin. Made with shea butter and baobab oil to soften, restore, and protect. Thaayu is Kikuyu for peace — a quiet name for everyday care.

  • Key ingredientsShea butter, baobab oil, mango butter
  • SkinAll skin types, especially dry
  • How to useMassage a small amount into clean, dry skin. Use daily.
