THAAYU

Body Butter

120 ml

Deep nourishment. Lasting moisture.

A rich, creamy blend that deeply moisturizes and nourishes the skin. Made with shea butter, baobab oil, and mango butter to soften, restore, and protect. Thaayu is Kikuyu for peace — a quiet name for everyday care.

What's inside

  • Shea butter

    Rich and creamy. Softens dry skin and helps hold moisture through the day.

  • Baobab oil

    A light, restoring oil from the baobab tree. Helps the butter settle without heaviness.

  • Mango butter

    Velvety on the skin, a close companion to shea for everyday body care.

We keep claims modest. Full cosmetic (INCI) lists will sit here when jars are prepared for sale.

How to use

After bathing, while skin is still slightly damp, warm a small amount between your palms. Massage into dry areas — elbows, knees, shins, hands — until the butter settles. Use daily. A little goes far.
