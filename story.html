Our Story

A little piece of home.

Shamba means “farm” in Swahili. It is more than a place — it is a way of life.

SHAMBA was inspired by what Eva’s family calls their home in Kenya — “The Shamba.” Growing up, days were spent among fertile soil, ancient trees, and the wisdom of generations. Mothers and grandmothers cared for skin with simple, honest ingredients. That ritual is the heart of the brand.

We make small-batch skincare by hand, with plants that have long been part of Kenyan home care: shea, baobab, marula, beeswax. The work is unhurried. Each jar is meant for everyday use, not for spectacle.

THAAYU takes its name from Kikuyu for peace. WENDO means love. The names are a quiet instruction: nourish the skin, and keep a little of home close.

When you choose SHAMBA, you are supporting a small business, local craft, and the traditions that have cared for generations. Asante sana.
